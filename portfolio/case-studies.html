6-Engineer Cross-Functional Team 4 Corporate Divisions AWS Enterprise Platform AI / ML Pipeline
The Problem

The organization needed an AWS-hosted enterprise data and systems integration platform capable of powering AI-driven predictive modeling pipelines — but four corporate divisions were operating with fragmented infrastructure ownership and no unified visibility into delivery health. Without systematic change governance, every release cycle carried real risk of unauthorized scope changes degrading platform integrity.

My Role

I owned end-to-end program delivery — not coordination, but the governance infrastructure itself: designing the automated risk-tracking system, running the Change Control Board, building real-time dashboards, and acting as the translation layer between systems architecture complexity and executive decision-making across all four divisions.

What I Did
  • Designed and operated an automated risk-tracking system across the 6-engineer team, creating systematic early-warning on delivery blockers — reducing them by 14%
  • Engineered automated regression testing protocols, increasing AWS infrastructure deployment velocity by 16%
  • Built real-time dashboards giving executive leadership live visibility into pipeline health and delivery metrics across all phases
  • Owned the Change Control Board (CCB) for all infrastructure-impacting requests — fully preventing unauthorized scope creep across every release cycle
  • Translated complex systems architecture and integration dependencies into milestone-level reporting, unifying 4 divisions around a single infrastructure roadmap
Outcome

Measurable improvements across every dimension of program health. Delivery blockers reduced 14% system-wide. Deployment velocity up 16%. Full architectural integrity maintained with zero unauthorized scope escalations. Four divisions held to a single roadmap throughout the program lifecycle — with live executive visibility at every stage.

Technical Project Manager — Systems Integration
Apr 2023 – Oct 2025
Anonymized Sample
8 Business Divisions Legacy-to-Cloud Migration API Systems Integration Vendor SLA Governance
The Problem

Eight business divisions were running fragmented legacy technical systems with no unified cloud infrastructure. Migration carried concentrated risk: API and system-integration conflicts that, if caught only at go-live, would trigger expensive post-deployment remediation. A critical 3-week delay mid-program further threatened both delivery timelines and baseline budget.

My Role

Program-manage the full AWS cloud migration across all 8 divisions — owning the technical risk matrix, vendor delivery lifecycles, sprint governance, and executive reporting. My mandate: hit every cutover deadline, prevent post-go-live remediation, and recover any delay without budget overrun.

What I Did
  • Maintained a technical risk matrix that caught critical API and system-integration conflicts before go-live — preventing $40K+ in post-deployment remediation costs
  • Governed all external vendor relationships end-to-end, holding every vendor to contracted SLA delivery timelines through milestone tracking
  • Maintained uninterrupted infrastructure deployment pipelines through high-volume system cutovers by partnering directly with engineers on sprint planning and release governance
  • Recovered a critical 3-week program delay by restructuring resource allocation matrices and re-sequencing infrastructure verification sprints — with zero increase to baseline budget
Outcome

Every cutover deadline across all 8 divisions hit ahead of schedule. Pre-go-live risk management prevented over $40K in remediation costs. The 3-week mid-program delay fully recovered with zero budget overrun. All external vendors held to contractual SLAs throughout the 2.5-year program engagement.

The Problem

Government sponsor MITRE required a custom AWS-based infrastructure and data systems pipeline delivered to production on a fixed, non-negotiable timeline. A 4-person engineering team left zero slack — every scope decision, milestone commitment, and sprint sequence had to be precise. Any drift would directly compress delivery time with no recovery buffer.

My Role

Full-lifecycle program management for a government-sponsored infrastructure delivery: scope governance, milestone planning, sprint cadence, and delivery reporting — with dual accountability to MITRE's technical requirements and GMU's program standards simultaneously.

What I Did
  • Directed full-lifecycle execution across the 4-person engineering team — from requirements scoping through production deployment
  • Governed scope boundaries rigorously throughout the program, protecting the fixed delivery date from any drift or creep
  • Built and maintained milestone reporting dashboards that provided the government sponsor real-time delivery progress visibility
  • Maintained sprint velocity by proactively removing blockers and managing team cadence under strict timeline constraints
Outcome

Custom AWS data systems pipeline delivered to production ahead of MITRE's fixed schedule. 100% of all committed milestones hit. Zero scope creep throughout the program. This engagement established the governance discipline — precise scope control, dashboard-driven accountability — that defines every program I have led since.
